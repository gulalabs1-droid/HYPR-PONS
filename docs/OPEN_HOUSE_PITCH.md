# Open House pitch

## Two-minute version

Onchain ecosystems are good at creating assets and transactions, but poor at connecting a live launch to a durable community. A new token may exist on a launchpad, an explorer, a chart site, and a chat link—without one place where the creator can go live and the community can understand what is happening.

HYPR is building that live launchpad for Robinhood Chain. A creator creates through HYPR, goes live, trades on a transparent bonding curve, earns the configured creator share, and takes the community through graduation to Uniswap v4. Pons is our first integration and market reference, so existing activity can still become a room while HYPR proves its native protocol loop.

In Discover, people can see what is Trending, Rising, New, or generating the most social activity. In a room, they can see the token address and onchain context, chat, follow, react, and share. HYPE is transparent social momentum—not financial advice, token quality, or a prediction.

The important loop is simple: create, go live, trade, creator earns, HYPR earns, curve progresses, graduate, and keep the room as the community home. A legitimate creator can prove control through wallet verification rather than typing an address into a form.

We are not building a black-box casino, a custom DEX, or a native streaming stack. We are building a transparent launch protocol plus the live social surface that makes onchain activity understandable and participatory.

## Five-minute expansion

- Show the fragmentation problem with a token address, explorer, chart, and chat links.
- Show how the HYPR room persists even when nobody is streaming.
- Explain bounded, idempotent event indexing and the planned Supabase persistence layer.
- Explain why Robinhood Chain is the primary ecosystem and why pons is the first integration.
- Show the honest boundary: fixtures are labeled, RPC failures are visible, and no contract or claim is called production-ready before verification.
- Close with the validation target: can real Robinhood Chain communities bring genuine users to their HYPR rooms without paid acquisition?
