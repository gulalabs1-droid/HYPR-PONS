# HYPR founder pitch — target 90–110 seconds, hard limit 2 minutes

Read in your own voice. This script does not invent credentials, users or revenue.
Use your preferred name in the opening. This is a presentation, not the separate
product demo. Record a simple take; the form says nothing fancy is required.

Hi, I'm the founder of Gula Labs, and I'm building HYPR, a live launchpad for
Robinhood Chain.

Today, an onchain creator can launch a token and still have no clear home for
the community. People jump between a launchpad, an explorer, trading charts,
and separate social and streaming apps. HYPR's idea is simple: every launch
should begin a lasting room where the creator and community can meet.

Our prototype connects fixed-supply token creation, transparent bonding-curve
trading, and a browser-native creator studio. It runs on Robinhood testnet,
with public evidence for creation, buying, selling, and fee collection. When a
launch confirms, HYPR recovers its real onchain room instead of leaving the
creator to search an explorer.

I'm focused on an approachable experience and shipping against real feedback.
The next step is a small creator pilot: help creators launch, observe what their
viewers need, and measure repeat visits, successful broadcasts, and operating
costs before spending on growth.

We are not claiming mainnet readiness or traction we haven't earned. Shared
chat and follows are previews today. Uniswap v4 graduation has fork-test proof,
but not a completed public-chain graduation. We also disclose the bootstrap
work that existed before this competition.

The long-term business hypothesis is a transparent protocol fee shared with
creators, supporting a useful room rather than just a one-time launch. Security,
moderation, and streaming economics are gates before a commercial rollout.

HYPR's goal is to make onchain communities easier to start and easier to stay in.
